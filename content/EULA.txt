4. USAGE COUNTS - WHAT THE SOFTWARE SENDS US

4.1 To count how many installations are in use, the Software sends a small
    message to our servers about once a day while it runs (a "usage ping").
    A usage ping contains only:

      - a random installation identifier that the Software generates the
        first time it runs and keeps in its own settings file;
      - the operating system family (Windows, macOS, or Linux) and processor
        architecture;
      - the operating system version; and
      - the Software's version.

4.2 The installation identifier is random. It is not derived from your
    hardware, your name, your user or computer name, or your account, and a
    usage ping does not carry your account or any credential. We do not
    store your IP address with usage pings.

4.3 The Software does not send us the content of your notifications, your
    prompts, code, files, file paths, project or agent names, usage
    statistics, crash dumps, or anything else from your computer as part of
    usage counting.

4.4 You can turn usage pings off at any time: in Settings on Windows, on
    the About page of the web interface, by setting "usagePingsEnabled":
    false in the Software's configuration file (config.json), or by setting
    the environment variable NOKOO_USAGE_PINGS=0. Turning them off does not
    affect any other feature.
